About MAN1K

MAN1K emerged from Khmelnytskyi, Ukraine in February 2023, crafting witch house as sonic ritual rather than entertainment. Each track is a portal - heavy reverb, pitched vocals, industrial shadows that demand you feel rather than listen. This is music that refuses compromise, that engages the full spectrum of human darkness and beauty simultaneously. Shaped by Ukrainian underground culture and years immersed in Japan, now based in Europe, MAN1K channels these cross-cultural currents into sound that evokes dissociation, melancholy, ritualistic tension.

From bedroom to stage, from solo artist to scene architect. The NIGHTSHADE TOUR transformed intimate venues into ceremonial spaces. CHERN Tokyo bridged continents and underground communities. DeadRay explores new territories with vocalist Lakomi. As administrator of #UWHR (Ukrainian Witch House Movement), MAN1K rebuilds infrastructure connecting Ukrainian, Japanese, Russian, and European scenes. "My reality and my path" - authenticity over calculation, ritual over performance, transformation over consumption.
