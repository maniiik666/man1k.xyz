⚔ GAME MECHANICS

Enemies spawn in waves. Every 3 waves the level increases — enemies get tougher, fire faster, and new enemy types appear.

▲ BOSS appears every 5 waves with 3 phases. Boss gets faster and unlocks new attack patterns each phase. Phase 3 includes teleportation and bullet spirals.

COMBO system: kill enemies in rapid succession to build combos. x5 and x10 combos give screen flash + bonus points. Combo multiplier boosts ALL kill points.

STREAK bonus: reach a 10-kill streak without getting hit to earn an auto-shield.

DASH gives brief invincibility frames — use it to dodge dense bullet patterns.

PRO TIPS: Weapons drop randomly with limited ammo — save LASER for bosses since beams pierce. SCATTER is best for clearing dense waves. HOMING is great when you need to focus on dodging. Dash through tight bullet spreads — the i-frames save lives. Keep your combo alive for massive score multipliers! Reach wave 30 to clear the signal.
