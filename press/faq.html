FAQ
What genre is MAN1K?
Witch-house and dark electronic. The project sits at the intersection of underground club music, industrial textures, and atmospheric electronics — rooted in the witch-house tradition but not constrained by it. For a full genre overview, see the genre brief.
What is Witch House music?
Witch house is a microgenre of underground electronic music that emerged in the late 2000s — characterized by slowed tempos, heavily processed vocals, occult-influenced aesthetics, and a collision of hip-hop, shoegaze, and industrial textures. For a detailed breakdown: Genre Brief — Witch House & MAN1K.
Where is MAN1K based?
Ukraine.
When, where and why did MAN1K start making music?
The project started in Ukraine in 2023. Witch house offered a sonic space that felt like the only honest one: sufficiently dark, sufficiently strange, sufficiently its own thing. It wasn't a calculated genre choice — the sound came first, the name for it came later.
Why are releases so infrequent?
Each release has to earn its place. The process is slow by design — writing, stripping back, rebuilding from scratch, mixing until nothing else can be removed. Output without purpose isn't output. When something is ready, it ships.
Does MAN1K use AI in their work?
AI tools are used strictly for technical tasks and routine production management — nothing creative. In composition, sound design, arrangement, mixing, and mastering, the work is entirely human. This isn't a trend position; it's a foundational one. Formally declared with the Human Intelligence Institute: view certificate.
