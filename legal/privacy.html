Analytics & Tracking

DNT / GPC Behavior

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and the TikTok pixel are disabled entirely. Only privacy-preserving Simple Analytics continues to run.

When DNT/GPC is not active, the following analytics tools may run:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — page views, session data, referral sources. Configured with IP anonymization and limited data retention.
  • Simple Analytics — cookieless, privacy-first analytics. No personal data collected. Privacy policy →
  • TikTok Pixel — loaded for advertising and audience-matching purposes (retargeting, Lookalike Audiences, Advanced Matching using hashed contact data). Disabled entirely when DNT/GPC is active. TikTok Technology Limited acts as an independent data controller for data it receives — see Cookie Policy, Section 03.1 for full detail.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — served via Cloudflare's CDN; cookieless, collects only aggregate traffic metrics.

Newsletter (Email)

The /subscribe page uses Buttondown to manage the mailing list. When you subscribe, your email address is transmitted to and stored by Buttondown under their own privacy policy. No email data is retained by man1k.xyz servers directly — Buttondown is the sole processor and data store for this list.

Beyond your email address, Buttondown records the following as part of standard newsletter operation:

  • An internal subscriber ID assigned automatically on signup
  • Engagement analytics — opens, clicks, and reply activity for each newsletter sent
  • Your email domain (e.g. gmail.com), open rate, click rate, and current subscription status (active / unsubscribed / bounced)
  • Internal notes may occasionally be attached to a subscriber record for list-management purposes

You can unsubscribe at any time via the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter email, or by sending a direct request to legal@man1k.xyz. Unsubscribing stops engagement tracking and updates your status; you may separately request full deletion of your subscriber record via the same channels.

03.1 //

Phone Number Database (SMS)

Separately from the email newsletter, MAN1K (Yaroslav Boruk) maintains a phone contact database used to send SMS updates about releases, live shows, and merchandise. Unlike the newsletter, this database is compiled directly by the artist from multiple sources over time rather than a single online opt-in form.

The following fields may be stored per contact: phone number, country, name, city, and source (a note on how the contact was acquired).

Processor & Storage

The database is hosted in the address book of AlphaSMS (alphasms.ua), an SMS delivery platform, under account/contract no. 118134. Before sending, numbers may be checked via an HLR (Home Location Register) lookup — a standard telecom verification confirming whether a number is currently active and reachable, used to reduce failed deliveries. This check does not add any field to a contact's stored record beyond those listed above.

Separately, AlphaSMS generates a report for each SMS campaign sent, containing:

  • Recipient country and mobile operator (per campaign, not stored on individual contact records)
  • Click rate on links included in the message
  • Campaign start and end time
  • A Mailing ID identifying that specific send

Opt-out: you can stop receiving messages at any time by blocking the sender ID on your phone, or by requesting removal directly at legal@man1k.xyz. Removal requests are honored by deleting the contact from the AlphaSMS address book.

AlphaSMS is operated by Interconnect Solutions (InterConnect Solutions Pte. Ltd., Singapore), which publishes its own GDPR-referencing privacy policy →. That policy states Interconnect Solutions acts as a data processor for contact data supplied by its customers and does not use it for any purpose other than the one the customer specifies.

03.2 //

Backups & Processor Transitions

In addition to the copies held by Buttondown and AlphaSMS, MAN1K (Yaroslav Boruk) retains a local backup of the email newsletter and SMS phone contact data, kept in a secure location. This backup exists for two reasons:

  • To be able to fulfil GDPR data-subject requests (access, rectification, erasure) independently of the processor's own systems and availability
  • To allow the contact list to be migrated if MAN1K ever switches to a different newsletter or SMS processor, without losing the ability to reach existing subscribers

If a processor migration takes place, affected subscribers will be notified by email or SMS, including a link or contact address to unsubscribe from the new processor. A deletion request made under Section 08, Your Rights removes your data from this local backup as well as from the active processor.

Local Storage

This site stores the following keys in localStorage for functional purposes. No personal data is included in any of these keys, and none is transmitted externally.

  • _m1k_v1 — aesthetic visit counter (homepage only; alters a decorative text element after 3+ visits; not suppressed by DNT)
  • mk_r — rate-limit event counter (cleared on rate window expiry)
  • mk_b — rate-ban expiry timestamp (cleared when ban expires)
  • vxb — game high score fallback when IndexedDB is unavailable (/game/ only)
All localStorage entries are stored only in your browser and are never transmitted externally. They can be cleared at any time via your browser's developer tools ("Application" → "Local Storage") or by clearing all site data for man1k.xyz.
